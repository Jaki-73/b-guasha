Frequently Asked

What exactly is gua sha?

Gua sha is a traditional East Asian healing technique that uses a smooth-edged tool to gently scrape the skin. On the face it lifts and de-puffs; on the body it releases tension and boosts circulation. [Edit to match how you describe it.]

Does it hurt? Will I bruise?

Facial gua sha is gentle and relaxing with no marks. Body gua sha uses firmer pressure and may leave temporary redness (called "sha") that fades within a few days — this is normal and part of the healing process. [Adjust to your technique.]
